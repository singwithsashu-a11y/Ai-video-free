Choose the engine that matches your setup.

Cloud AI

Recommended for quality and speed. Configure AI_BASE_URL, AI_API_KEY and AI_MODEL in Render. Use a model that supports vision if you want photo chat.

Ollama

Works only if an Ollama server is reachable from your backend. Use a vision model for photo questions.

Browser AI

Downloads a small model to your phone. It can take minutes and may fail on low-RAM devices; use only as a fallback.
